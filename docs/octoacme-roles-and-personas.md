# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Lead** on test strategy and acceptance criteria validation
- Receive technical direction from **Technical Architect/Tech Lead** on design and architecture
- Coordinate with **DevOps/Release Manager** on deployment and infrastructure needs
- Support **Project Managers** with accurate estimates and status updates

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **QA/Testing Lead** to define acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Collaborate with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on business priorities and success metrics
- Partner with **Technical Architect/Tech Lead** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Report outcomes and metrics to **Stakeholders/Sponsors**

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator** on team ceremonies and process adherence
- Report status and escalate blockers to **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Track quality metrics and test progress with **QA/Testing Lead**
- Work with **DevOps/Release Manager** on deployment schedules and readiness
- Communicate timelines and dependencies with **Technical Architect/Tech Lead**

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure releases meet quality standards. They collaborate with developers and product leads to maintain high test coverage and customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for features and releases
- Write and maintain automated test suites
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Report quality metrics and test coverage
- Identify and document defects, regressions, and edge cases
- Coordinate manual testing and user acceptance testing (UAT) when needed
- Work with developers on test design and coverage planning

### Goals
- Catch issues before production deployment
- Maintain high test coverage and quality standards
- Reduce defect escape rate and post-release incidents
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Definition of Done reviews and acceptance criteria validation
- Test strategy discussions during planning
- Defect reports and quality metrics in status updates
- QA sign-off on release readiness
- Collaboration with developers on test implementation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** to define testable acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Review acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** to ensure quality expectations are clear
- Report quality readiness to **Project Managers** and **DevOps/Release Manager** for release decisions
- Coordinate UAT activities with **Stakeholders/Sponsors**

---

## Technical Architect/Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects/Tech Leads provide technical direction, conduct design reviews, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system architecture. They mentor developers and help identify technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers on coding standards and best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Ensure solutions are scalable and maintainable
- Collaborate on performance and security considerations
- Contribute to code reviews and technical documentation
- Advise on technology selection and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Prevent architectural mistakes that are costly to fix later
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and RFC (Request for Comment) discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Architecture and scalability assessments
- Technical risk identification in planning and standups

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on technical direction and best practices through code reviews and design discussions
- Advise **Project Managers** on technical feasibility, complexity, and timeline estimates
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on feasibility of features and technical trade-offs
- Work with **Security/Compliance Officer** on security architecture and risk mitigation
- Partner with **DevOps/Release Manager** on deployment strategy and infrastructure requirements

---

## Scrum Master/Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Delivery Facilitators support the team in following agreed processes, remove blockers, and facilitate ceremonies. They coach the team on agile practices and help maintain a healthy, productive delivery cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Ensure the team follows defined processes and best practices
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track team velocity and identify trends
- Support continuous improvement initiatives

### Goals
- Maintain a predictable and sustainable delivery pace
- Foster psychological safety and open communication
- Help the team continuously improve their processes
- Reduce friction and maximize team focus on delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective discussions and action item tracking
- Blocker resolution and escalations to **Project Managers**
- Team coaching and process improvement suggestions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Support **Project Managers** in coordinating team activities and managing dependencies
- Coach **Developers** on agile practices and sustainable pace
- Help **Product Managers** prioritize backlog and refine requirements with the team
- Escalate organizational blockers to **Project Managers** for resolution

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide business context, strategic guidance, and approvals. They represent business priorities, customer needs, and organizational constraints. Sponsors champion the project and ensure alignment with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and provide organizational support
- Communicate project status and outcomes to leadership
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy

### Goals
- Deliver business value and ROI
- Ensure projects align with organizational strategy
- Minimize organizational risks and disruptions
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and support

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and major milestone reviews
- Monthly status updates and business metrics reporting
- Executive briefings and decision gates
- Approval of scope changes and resource requests

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** to ensure project alignment with business objectives
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on business priorities, success metrics, and trade-offs
- Communicate business constraints to the delivery team via **Project Managers**
- Review project outcomes with **Product Managers** and identify next steps
- Coordinate with **Security/Compliance Officer** on compliance and risk requirements

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure security requirements, compliance standards, and risk mitigation are built into project delivery. They review designs, validate compliance, and help prevent security and regulatory issues.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review technical designs for security and compliance implications
- Conduct security assessments and vulnerability reviews
- Ensure regulatory and compliance requirements are met
- Coordinate incident response and security escalations
- Provide security guidance and best practices to the team

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build security into every phase of delivery
- Reduce organizational risk and regulatory exposure
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning
- Design review collaboration with technical team
- Compliance validation before release
- Security incident escalations and post-incident reviews
- Risk register updates for security and compliance issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Technical Architect/Tech Lead** on security architecture and design reviews
- Review **Developers'** code and implementations for security best practices
- Work with **QA/Testing Lead** to ensure security testing is included in test plans
- Report security and compliance risks to **Project Managers** and **Stakeholders/Sponsors**
- Advise **Product Managers** on compliance and security trade-offs in feature design

---

## DevOps/Release Manager

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Managers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release coordination. They enable fast, reliable, and predictable releases while maintaining system stability and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and infrastructure
- Coordinate release planning and deployment schedules
- Manage environment setup (dev, staging, production)
- Monitor system health and performance post-release
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response
- Automate deployment and infrastructure processes
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and frequent releases
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Maintain system stability and performance
- Reduce manual effort and human error in deployments

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with **QA/Testing Lead** and **Project Managers**
- Deployment schedules and coordination with stakeholders
- Post-deployment monitoring and incident communications
- Infrastructure and performance updates to the team

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate release timing and readiness with **Project Managers** and **QA/Testing Lead**
- Work with **Developers** on deployment requirements and infrastructure needs
- Partner with **Technical Architect/Tech Lead** on infrastructure design and performance optimization
- Collaborate with **Security/Compliance Officer** on secure deployment practices and compliance
- Report deployment status and system health to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** via **Project Managers**
